# 5. User Journey

## Overview

ArenaMind AI is designed to assist users throughout the complete event lifecycle rather than only during the match.

The platform provides contextual assistance before arrival, during entry, inside the stadium, throughout the event, during emergencies, and after the match ends.

Instead of acting as a reactive chatbot, ArenaMind AI proactively supports users at every stage of their journey.

# Phase 1 — Before Leaving Home

## User Goals

- Check match timing
- Find transportation
- Understand stadium rules
- Prepare for arrival

### AI Responsibilities

- Show weather
- Suggest departure time
- Recommend transport
- Display prohibited items
- Share parking guidance
- Explain gate timings

### Example

User:

> "When should I leave?"

AI:

> "Considering today's traffic, you should leave by 4:10 PM to reach Gate 3 comfortably before kickoff."

# Proactive Assistance

ArenaMind AI does not wait for users to ask every question.

Examples:

"You are approaching your gate."

"Kickoff starts in 15 minutes."

"The nearest restroom is on your right."

"Heavy crowd detected near Exit A."

"Would you like a quieter exit?"

These proactive moments reduce stress and improve the overall experience.

---

# User Journey Principles

Every stage follows five principles.

## 1. Minimal Interaction

Users should never perform unnecessary actions.

---

## 2. Context Awareness

The assistant remembers previous interactions.

---

## 3. One Conversation

Users continue the same conversation throughout the event.

No switching between different services.

---

## 4. Assistance Before Search

ArenaMind AI recommends actions instead of simply displaying information.

---

## 5. Calm Experience

Even during emergencies the assistant should remain calm, clear, and reassuring.

# Journey Success Criteria

A successful ArenaMind AI experience means the user can attend an entire event—from home to home again—without ever needing to search through menus or multiple applications.

The AI should remain the single point of interaction throughout the complete event lifecycle.
